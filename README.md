# 🪄 MuggleProof Luggage Companion

*An enchanted web experience for the discerning witch or wizard traveler.*

Welcome, magical friends, to the digital portal for **MuggleProof Luggage**! This repository holds the incantations and enchantments (HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS) for a truly whimsical, mysterious, and cinematic Hogwarts-inspired travel companion.

---

## ✨ Magical Features Unveiled

* **The Marauder's Map Tracker**: Instead of a mundane muggle GPS, your dashboard features a living parchment canvas. Watch as tiny footprints walk the Hogwarts corridors, revealing the location of your enchanted trunk.
* **Audio Synthesis Spells**: Delight in the subtle crackle of a warm common-room fireplace, the tap of a wand when hovering over parchment menus, and triumphant arpeggios when you cast successful spells (or successfully submit forms!). 
* **Alohomora Form Charms**: Ensure your inputs are correct, lest you be met with a dissonant buzz and a physical shake of the login lock!
* **House Sorting Magic**: Unsure which luggage suits you best? The Sorting Hat will decide! Answer honestly, and watch as the entire interface transfigures to match the colors of your newly assigned house (Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw, or Hufflepuff).
* **Interactive Dobby**: Dobby the House Elf resides in the Purchases section. Click on him to hear him squeak with joy, or watch his excitement bubble over when you add items to your cart!
* **Enchanted Glass Slots**: Missing an image? Fear not! Our item slots are shielded by powerful charms, displaying glowing velvet and gold magic circles in place of mundane muggle photographs.

---

## 📜 How to Cast the Spells (Run the Site)

This project requires no complex muggle build steps or package managers. It is pure, unadulterated web magic.

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your favorite web browser.
3. Keep your wand hand ready, and make sure your volume is turned up to hear the magical chimes!

---

## 🧙‍♂️ Code Architecture

- **`magic-common.js`**: The core spellbook. Handles the cursor sparkle trails, page-wipe transitions, and the `MagicEngine` audio synthesizer.
- **`magic-common.css`**: Global enchantments, including custom fonts (`Cinzel`, `Poppins`), vintage gradients, and animation keyframes.
- **Page-Specific Charms**:
  - `homepage.js`: Governs the Alohomora login logic and floating SVG animations.
  - `features.js`: Houses the logic for the Marauder's Map canvas and Sorting Hat integrations.
  - `purchase.js`: Contains Dobby's interactive behaviors, parchment modal reveals, and flying cart sparks.
  - `quiz.js`: Calculates your perfect magical luggage match and transfigures the site theme.
  - `contact.js`: Handles Owl Post dispatch validations and custom success banners.
